Put your finished images in this folder. Each one replaces its drawn placeholder automatically.
Missing files are fine: the drawn version stays.

File names (use .webp for smallest size, or .png / .jpg):

bg        16:9, opaque. Wall, window, poster, floor. Suggested 3200 x 1800.
mid       16:9, TRANSPARENT png/webp. Bed, chair, desk, lamp, the dark under the desk.
          Paint the lamp light INTO this image; the drawn glow is hidden once mid exists.
fg        16:9, TRANSPARENT. Dark foreground shapes and floor shadow. Keep it sparse.
mimi      transparent. Sitting under the desk, head down. Ratio about 140 : 145. Suggested 560 x 580.
figurine  transparent. The toy astronaut, standing. Ratio 48 : 68. Suggested 192 x 272.

Keep the same camera: the room must line up with the drawn one (desk in the centre, window on the left,
bed on the right), because the camera shots are aimed at those places.
