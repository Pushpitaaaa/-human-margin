Put your own sound files in this folder. Each replaces its built-in sound automatically.

hum      looping room tone, plays quietly under the whole game. Make it a seamless loop, 30 to 60 seconds.
slide    the figurine sliding out from under the desk (1 to 2 seconds).
tick     the figurine stopping (under half a second).
rustle   the Commander crouching down (about 1 second).
car      the car arriving outside (3 to 4 seconds).
launch   the launch rumble (6 to 8 seconds).

Use .mp3, .ogg or .wav. Missing files are fine: the built-in sound stays.
